In April I had the wonderful opportunity of attending and presenting at EVOLANG XVI in Plovdiv, Bulgaria. This was a great challenge for me, as it was my first oral presentation at a major international conference. You could catch me presenting work from two projects:

1. Sibierska, M., Karkowska, K., Ćwiek, A., Placiński, M., Wacewicz, S., Żyndul, A., Żywiczyński, P. “What exactly is aligned in the case of alignment in whole-body communication? Case studies of pantomimic enactments”

2. Boruta, M., Żyndul, A., Placiński, M. “First observe then perform - children’s changing gestural comprehension between the ages of 2 and 5” (poster)

More information (as well as the book of abstracts!) can be found here: https://sites.google.com/york.ac.uk/evolang2026/home
