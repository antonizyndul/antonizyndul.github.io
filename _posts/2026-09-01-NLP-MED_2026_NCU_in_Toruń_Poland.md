I presented work from my MA degree at the NLP-Med conference organised by the Centre for Language Evolution Studies in Toruń, Poland. The presentation focused on tools (Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count - LIWC) and resources (Distress Analysis Interview Corpus Wizard of Oz - DAIC-WOZ) which can be useful when attempting to learn about psychological states through the medium of language.
