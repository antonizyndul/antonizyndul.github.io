Studying how humans make meaning beyond words.

I am a student researcher at Nicolaus Copernicus University (NCU) in Toruń, Poland, working in the intersection of cognitive science and linguistics. My research focuses on Polish Sign Language (PJM), gesture, and corpus linguistics — exploring the kinematic continuum between lexical signing, constructed action, and pantomime.

My work is grounded in hands-on empirical methods: video corpora, motion capture data, and computational annotation pipelines. I am particularly interested in language evolution, non-verbal communication, and sign language studies.

PhD Project

Constructed Action in Polish Sign Language

Investigating the kinematic continuum between lexical signing, constructed action (CA), and pantomime in PJM. The project examines iconicity, transparency, and conventionalization, drawing on motion capture data and gesture theory.
